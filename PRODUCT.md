# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Technical hiring managers, engineering leads, and startup founders evaluating a Full Stack AI Engineer for their team or company. Also visited by peers and collaborators assessing open-source work.

## Product Purpose

A premium portfolio that establishes Venu Gopal Reddy Palugulla as a top-tier Full Stack AI Engineer. Demonstrates production AI capability through live metrics, case studies, and interactive demos. Converts visitors into interview requests and collaboration offers.

## Positioning

Not a resume-in-browser. An experience that proves AI engineering skill through its own construction — the medium is the message. Every visual element, interaction, and metric on the page demonstrates the same craft the owner brings to production systems.

## Operating Context

Viewed on desktop and mobile, often in low-light evening browsing (OLED dark mode primary). Hiring managers scan in 30-60 seconds; deeper evaluation happens on case studies and GitHub links. Page must load fast, work offline-capable, and respect reduced motion preferences.

## Brand Commitments

- Purple primary identity (#7C3AED family). Existing color associations carry forward.
- Technical precision without costume — no faux-terminal aesthetics or monospace-as-identity.
- English only, professional register, direct without hype.

## Facts and Evidence

- DM Sans + Space Grotesk font stack proven in current deployment
- Vercel-hosted, custom domain
- Existing metrics: 89% CNN accuracy, sub-100ms inference, 60% query latency reduction
- Git-based workflow, TypeScript + React + Tailwind v4 stack
